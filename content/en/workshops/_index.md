## Organisation

Training courses can be ordered as a complete package.

They can also be tailored to specific areas of interest:

* e.g. a workshop on the data queue concept to dive deeper into the subject – perhaps combined with open source products
* or a combination of the courses listed above

## Execution

The courses are largely hands-on.
I don't believe in just presenting pretty slides if, for various reasons, they can't be applied 1:1 in practice.
That's why every participant should have a PC/laptop at their disposal.

## Remote vs. on-site

Experience has shown that remote can also have advantages over the classic on-site approach:

* As participants can share their screen too, everyone can watch how someone else's problem gets solved.
* Easier to combine with daily business (see [Split over several days](#split-over-several-days)).

## Split over several days

With remote workshops, a course that would take 2 days can be split into 4 half days – even across several weeks.
This way daily business is blocked less.
