Here is an example of what modernisation, cost reduction and simplification of development can look like. 

[Coder](https://coder.com) is a tool for centralising development.  

* One-time configuration of the development environment (template)
* Workspace for new developers created with just a few clicks
* More security of the sources
* No dependency on the local PC (viruses, HW crash, etc.)  
  Your own workspace can be accessed from any PC.
* No complicated setup of the environment for each developer:
  * Firewall
  * Proxy
  * Local installations
  * etc.
